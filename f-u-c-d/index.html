F.U.C.D

Flat Ultralight Compass & Drink

Project
Eco-thought & materials course, Shenkar
Location
Ramat-Gan, IL
Year
2020

About the project

I wanted to design a compass that's eco friendly, simple & helpful.

A product that's built to use in nature, inspired by nature & for nature lovers.

It works just like a regular compass, but when in dire need you can drink the water & eat the transparent package, while keeping the compass body with you for future use.
