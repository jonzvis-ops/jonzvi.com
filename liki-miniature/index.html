Liki Miniature

A working 1:6 scale Liki that folds just like the real trike

Company
Doona
Role
Design, SolidWorks CAD, 3D printing, hand assembly
Year
2024–2025

About the project

The brief
A personal request from Doona's CEO: a desk-sized Liki that folds exactly like the full-size trike.
My part
Design, engineering and build. I modelled every part in SolidWorks, worked out the fold mechanism at 1:6 scale, then 3D-printed and assembled each model by hand.
Build
Several versions were made: some with metal hinges and axles, others fully printed in plastic.
Result
A fully working miniature that folds and unfolds like the real Liki. A one-off for the CEO, not a commercial product.
