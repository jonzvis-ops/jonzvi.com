Liki Miniature

One-off folding miniature of the Liki trike, made at the CEO's request

Company
Doona
Role
Design, SolidWorks CAD, 3D printing, hand assembly
Year
2022–now

About the project

The problem
A personal request from Doona's CEO: a miniature Liki trike that folds just like the real one.
My part
I designed and built a miniature model of Doona's Liki tricycle that folds just like the product.
How
Designed in SolidWorks, 3D-printed, and assembled by hand. I built a few versions: some with metal hinges and axles, some all plastic.
Result
A working miniature at about 1:6 scale, made for the CEO. It was never manufactured or sold.
